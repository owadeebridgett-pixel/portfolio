The problem

Cash flow suffers when debtor follow-up depends on a person remembering to do it.

Most South African SME finance departments follow up on overdue invoices verbally and inconsistently. No structured escalation. No record of what was sent. No visibility of the full debtor book. The Finance Manager only finds out how bad it is when they build the age analysis manually.

How DebtorIQ works

The right notice. The right debtor.
The right time. Every time.

1

Data Source

Invoice data lives in Google Sheets — DebtorIQ reads it automatically

DebtorIQ connects directly to the finance team's Google Sheet. Reads invoice number, client, contact email, dates, terms, amounts and status — then calculates days overdue per debtor based on their individual payment terms.
