The problem

Three documents. Zero system. Hours to days of work — every candidate.

A recruitment agency was manually processing every candidate across three disconnected sources — the candidate's original CV, the job description from the client, and notes from the interview. Each one lived in a different place. Every candidate profile was built from scratch by a consultant, taking hours to days of high-skill time on low-value work.

THE BOTTLENECK THIS SYSTEM SOLVES

How the system works

A connected operating system
for the entire candidate journey.

Every step in the process — from intake to branded output — happens inside one connected system. Here is exactly how it works.

1

Entry Point

Ursula — the monday.com AI Agent — directs the consultant

The system starts with Ursula — a custom monday.com AI Agent built specifically for this agency. Consultants interact with Ursula directly inside monday.com to trigger the CV generation process. Ursula guides them to the intake form — without leaving the platform.

Why an agent?

Instead of remembering where to go or what to do, consultants simply ask Ursula. The system comes to them — removing friction and ensuring the process is always started correctly.

Ursula — Candidate Profile Specialist — lives inside monday.com and guides consultants through the process

2

Information Capture

One form captures all three inputs at once

The monday.com form captures everything needed in one submission — the candidate's name, the job description (as a file upload), and the interview transcript. Three sources of truth, captured once, in one place.

The shift

Previously this information lived in three different places and had to be assembled manually. Now it enters the system once — and flows automatically from here.

The monday.com intake form — one submission captures all three inputs

The branded trigger button — sitting on the client's monday.com board

3

monday.com Operating System

The board becomes the candidate's record — populated automatically

Once the form is submitted, the candidate record is created in monday.com and all three documents are automatically attached. The board shows the full pipeline — every candidate, their inputs, their status and their output — in one view that the whole team can see.

What the board replaces

A shared folder full of inconsistently named files. An email inbox used as a filing system. A consultant's memory of where everything is.

4

Intelligence Layer

AI evaluates every candidate against the role — and scores them

This is where AI enters — and only here. The system reads all three documents together, evaluates the candidate against the specific role requirements, and writes structured outputs directly into the monday.com columns. No prompt engineering needed from the consultant. No copy-paste. Just structured, consistent intelligence applied to every candidate.

The bigger picture

This is not a CV tool.
It is a recruitment operating system.

The architecture built here — form capture, monday.com as the central record, AI applied at a specific step, branded document output — is the same architecture that works across every industry. The inputs and outputs change. The system logic does not.

What looks like a candidate profile generator is actually a demonstration of how any business process that currently runs on people, emails and spreadsheets can be turned into a connected, consistent, visible operating system.
