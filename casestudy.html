How the system works

A connected operating system
for the entire candidate journey.

1

Entry Point

Ursula — the monday.com AI Agent — directs the consultant

The system starts with Ursula — a custom monday.com AI Agent built for the agency. Consultants interact with Ursula directly inside monday.com to trigger the CV generation process, without leaving the platform.

Why an agent

Consultants ask Ursula. The system comes to them — removing friction and ensuring the process starts correctly every time.

Ursula — Candidate Profile Specialist — guides consultants

2

Information Capture

One form captures all three inputs at once

The monday.com form captures everything needed in one submission — the candidate's name, the job description as a file upload, and the interview transcript. Three sources of truth, captured once.

3

monday.com Board

The candidate record is populated automatically

Once the form is submitted, the candidate record is created in monday.com and all three documents are automatically attached. The board shows the full pipeline in one view.

What the board replaces

A shared folder of inconsistently named files. An email inbox used as a filing system. A consultant's memory of where everything is.

Intelligence Layer

AI evaluates every candidate against the role — and scores them

The system reads all three documents together, evaluates the candidate against role requirements, and writes structured outputs directly into monday.com columns. No prompt engineering. No copy-paste.

The bigger picture

The same architecture applies to finance administration.

Form capture, monday.com as central record, AI applied at a specific step, branded output. The architecture is the same across finance and operations. The inputs and outputs change.

This is why finance administration is the primary focus — DebtorIQ, AP automation, financial reporting — all built on the same methodology demonstrated here.
