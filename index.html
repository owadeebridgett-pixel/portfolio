The real cost

Finance admin eats hours that should go to analysis.

South African SME finance departments spend the majority of their time on tasks that should not require a skilled finance person. Following up on debtors. Rebuilding the same reports. Capturing receipts by hand. Copying data between systems. Automation removes every one of these from the workload — so the team focuses on decisions, not admin.

01

📇

Debtor follow-up is verbal and inconsistent.

No structured escalation. No record of contact. Overdue accounts slip through because follow-up depends on someone remembering. Cash flow suffers.

02

📊

The same reports are rebuilt from scratch every month.

Age analysis. Management accounts. Month-end pack. Pulled manually, formatted by hand, hours of work. The same document. Every month. From scratch.

03

👤

Month-end depends on the right person being in.

When that person is on leave, the process breaks. Finance operations should run on systems — not on individuals. Continuity is not optional.

What gets automated

Every repetitive task in the finance function can be systematised.

Finance automation does not replace the finance team. It removes the work that should never have landed there — so the team focuses on analysis, decisions and relationships.
