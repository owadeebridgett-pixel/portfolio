What Gets Built

Finance systems designed to replace manual admin.

Every system starts with a manual process and ends with something that runs automatically — structured, consistent and visible to management in real time. Finance administration is the primary focus. Business operations is secondary.

Finance Administration — Primary

📇Live

Accounts Receivable (DebtorIQ)

Automated notices at days 3, 10 and 17. Every contact logged. Weekly age analysis delivered automatically to the Finance Manager.

Replaces

Verbal follow-up · Manual Excel tracking · Ad hoc reporting

📥In Dev

Accounts Payable Automation

Invoice capture from email, supplier matching, approval routing and payment tracking without manual data entry.

Replaces

Manual capture · Email chains · Verbal approvals

📊In Dev

Automated Financial Reporting

Management accounts and month-end packs generated from accounting software on schedule — no manual rebuild.

Replaces

Manual month-end · Excel formatting · Late reports

📄On Roadmap

Client Statement Generation

Branded statements from live data, delivered to clients automatically on schedule.

Replaces

Manual statement runs · Inconsistent formatting

🔄On Roadmap

Reconciliation Automation

Bank recon, intercompany matching and exception flagging — without manual line-by-line work.

Replaces

Manual matching · Recon spreadsheets · Month-end delays

📋On Roadmap

Audit Pack Automation

Supporting schedules and documentation compiled automatically from live data for audit submission.

Replaces

Manual schedule compilation · Scattered documents

Business Operations — Secondary

👥Live

CV Generation System

CV, JD and transcript captured once. AI evaluates fit. Branded profile generated and filed in monday.com automatically.

👤On Roadmap

HR Onboarding Packs

New hire details captured once. Branded onboarding pack generated and delivered automatically.

📌On Roadmap

SOP & Compliance Docs

Process documentation generated from structured inputs — consistent, current and version-controlled.
