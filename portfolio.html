Automated debtor follow-up, escalation and age analysis for South African SMEs.

Debtor follow-up was verbal and inconsistent. Invoices tracked in Excel. Age analysis built manually every week. Cash flow suffered. DebtorIQ reads invoice data from Google Sheets, calculates days overdue per debtor against individual payment terms, fires the correct notice at days 3, 10 and 17, logs every contact, and delivers the weekly age analysis automatically to the Finance Manager.

“R2 839 150 total outstanding visibility — delivered to the Finance Manager's inbox every Monday morning before the week starts.”
